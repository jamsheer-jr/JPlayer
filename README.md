<h1 align="center">J Player</h1>

<p align="center">Your Telegram videos, on the phone in your hand and on the TV in your room.</p>

J Player is an Android media player for the videos in your own Telegram chats and channels.
Sign in with your own Telegram account and everything is already there: shelves for movies,
series, languages and genres, every quality a post carries as a copy you can switch between,
subtitles that can be translated as you watch, downloads for offline viewing, background play
and picture in picture.

## Install

1. Open the [Releases](https://github.com/jamsheer-jr/JPlayer/releases) page and download the
   newest `JPlayer-<version>-universal.apk`.
2. Open the file on the phone or the TV and allow the install when Android asks.
3. Open J Player and sign in with your Telegram account.

Each release installs over the one before it, keeping your place in what you were watching,
and the app offers its own update when a newer release is out.
